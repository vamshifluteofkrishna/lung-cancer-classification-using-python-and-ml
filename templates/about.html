Dataset Preprocessing & Hygiene

Standardization methodology reproducing the exploratory analysis

1. Missing Value Imputation

Mean imputation using Scikit-Learn's SimpleImputer(strategy='mean') was fitted on the training distributions for Alcohol use, Obesity, Smoking, and Weight Loss to eliminate missing values without introducing statistical distortion.

2. Categorical Mapping

Demographic features are mapped directly to numeric dimensions:
• Gender: Male → 0, Female → 1
• Level: Low → 0, Medium → 1, High → 2

3. Correlation Feature Selection

Features exhibiting high collinearity or duplicate properties (Smoking.1, Swallowing Difficulty.1, Genetic Risk, Occupational Hazards, and Patient Id) were dropped as specified in the original research notebook.

4. MinMax Scaling

Features are scaled to a [0, 1] range using MinMaxScaler() fitted exclusively on the X_train split to prevent data leakage during inference.
